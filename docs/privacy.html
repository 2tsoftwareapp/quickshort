Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026 · Tiếng Việt bên dưới

The short version

Quickshort runs on your machine. Screenshots, annotations, OCR results, and settings stay local. Three kinds of traffic exist: an image you share (to our share worker, or to your own Google Drive if you opt in), a bug report you send, and a daily update check against GitHub Releases. The app has no analytics, no tracking, and no accounts.

What stays on your device

  • Captures and saved PNGs (~/Pictures/Quickshort, or your configured directory)
  • Settings, hotkeys, and window preferences (~/.config/quickshort/)
  • OCR results. Recognition runs on your device: Apple Vision on macOS, Windows.Media.Ocr on Windows, Tesseract on Linux. No image or text goes to a cloud OCR service.

What leaves your device

Share link

Pressing Share uploads that one image, encoded as WebP, to a Cloudflare Worker (quickshort-share). The worker stores it in Cloudflare R2 (metadata in Workers KV) and returns a random 128-bit URL (/s/<id>). Anyone holding the link can view the image until it expires — or until you delete it yourself: every upload also mints a one-time delete key, kept on your device in shares.json. Right-click a shared thumbnail in the album and choose Revoke Link to take the image down early.

Limits are 12 MB per upload and 30 shares per IP per day. Shared images are not indexed or listed anywhere. The unguessable ID is the only protection, so treat a share link as public: fine for a screenshot you would post anyway, wrong for secrets.

Share via your Google Drive (optional)

If you turn on Share Links via Google Drive in the tray, Share signs you in with your own Google account (OAuth with PKCE, in your browser) and uploads the image to a Quickshort folder in your Drive, shared as "anyone with the link". The app asks only for the drive.file scope, so it can see and change only the files it created. The image counts against your Drive storage and stays until you delete it; sharing the same capture again revokes it by deleting the file. The refresh token is stored on your device in gdrive.json next to config.toml (readable only by your user). Disconnect Google Drive revokes it with Google and deletes the file. Google's privacy policy applies to data in your Drive; nothing passes through Quickshort servers.

Bug report

The report form sends your app version, OS and architecture, a diagnostics bundle, and your note to the quickshort-report worker, which files it as a public GitHub issue on 2tsoftwareapp/quickshort. The issue is visible to everyone and stays up, so keep secrets and private screenshots out of the note. Reports are limited to 10 per IP per day.

Update check

At most once a day the app queries api.github.com/repos/2tsoftwareapp/quickshort/releases/latest to compare versions. GitHub sees the request's IP and user-agent. Turn it off with auto_update = false in config.toml.

Third parties

  • Cloudflare runs the share and report workers and stores shared images. Its standard edge request logs apply.
  • Google stores images you share through your own Drive, if you opt in.
  • GitHub serves release downloads, update checks, public issues, and this site (GitHub Pages).
  • This website loads fonts from Google Fonts. The app itself loads no remote resources.

What we don't collect

No analytics, telemetry, or crash reporting unless you send a report yourself. No accounts and no email collection. Shared images are deleted on schedule, not kept. Nothing is sold or given to advertisers.

Your controls

  • Nothing uploads unless you press Share or send a bug report.
  • A shared link expires on its own. Deleting your local copy under shared/ does not affect it.
  • To remove a public bug report or a shared image early, open an issue on GitHub and it gets deleted.

Chính sách bảo mật

Mọi thứ nằm trên máy bạn: ảnh chụp, chú thích, kết quả OCR, cấu hình. Ra mạng chỉ khi bạn bấm Share, bấm gửi báo lỗi, hoặc lúc app tự hỏi GitHub xem có bản mới (một lần mỗi ngày). Ngoài ra không có analytics hay tài khoản gì.

  • Ảnh Share được nén WebP rồi up lên Cloudflare Worker, nằm trong Cloudflare R2 (metadata trong Workers KV) và tự xoá 30 ngày sau lần xem cuối (trần cứng 90 ngày). Link là một ID ngẫu nhiên 128-bit: ai có link đều xem được, nên đừng share thứ cần giữ kín. IP của bạn được ghi vào một khoá rate-limit sống 2 ngày, dùng để chặn quá 30 share mỗi ngày.
  • Nếu bật Share Links via Google Drive: bạn đăng nhập Google của chính mình, ảnh được tải vào thư mục Quickshort trong Drive của bạn (ai có link đều xem được), tốn dung lượng Drive của bạn và nằm đó tới khi bạn xoá. App chỉ xin quyền drive.file, chỉ thấy file do app tạo. Token lưu trên máy trong gdrive.json; Disconnect Google Drive thu hồi token và xoá file này.
  • Báo lỗi gồm version, OS, diagnostics và ghi chú bạn nhập, đăng thành một GitHub issue công khai. Đừng dán mật khẩu hay token vào đó.
  • Kiểm tra cập nhật là một request tới GitHub Releases; GitHub thấy IP của bạn. Muốn tắt thì đặt auto_update = false trong config.toml.
  • OCR chạy ngay trên máy (Vision trên macOS, Windows.Media.Ocr trên Windows, Tesseract trên Linux). Ảnh và chữ không đi đâu cả.
